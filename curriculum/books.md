## How I add a principle

1. Read something that changes a decision, not just something I agree with.
2. Close the book and write it in my own words. If I cannot, I did not get it.
3. Give it the next free `P<NN>` id, a source, tags, and a **Prompt** that could
   only be answered by looking at my actual behaviour this week.
4. Commit. It enters the rotation on the next run.
