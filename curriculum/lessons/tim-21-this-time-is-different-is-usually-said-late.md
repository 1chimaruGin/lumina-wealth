# "This time is different" is usually said late

"This time is different" surfaces near market peaks because it represents the moment when smart money has finished rewriting the story to justify current prices. By the time you hear it widely—from fund managers, business journalists, retail investors—the bull run has already priced in the new fundamentals. What's left is just extraction.

Reinhart and Rogoff examined financial crises across eight centuries and found that peak leverage and peak optimism always coincide. The crash does not come when everyone is nervous; it comes when everyone is *convinced*. "This time is different" is that conviction. In the dot-com bubble, VCs and funds genuinely believed the internet changed capitalism forever—and it did, just not at the valuations they paid. The Nikkei peaked at ¥38,915 in December 1989 on the belief that Japanese companies had escaped the boom-bust cycle. It fell over 50% in the decade that followed.

The phrase marks a transition: from discovery of genuine fundamentals to justification of prices that have overshot them. Early in a bull run, the phrase doesn't exist—fundamentals are weak, the story is uncertain. Middle stages see the genuine insight emerge. Late stages see the phrase everywhere because it is the *last argument* before everyone realizes valuation still matters.

For you as an observer: the phrase signals that expected future returns have compressed. The bull run has already captured the upside. Crashes follow, but not reliably on a timetable. Bitcoin fell 76% from its November 2021 peak and took over two years to recover to previous highs.

Hearing "this time is different" widely means you are late to the trend, not early. That's all the timing signal tells you.

**Key idea.** When everyone says 'this time is different', the bull run's upside is already priced in and expected returns have collapsed.

**Hard truth.** The phrase appears at tops because smart money needs new buyers to sell to. You can recognize the signal but profit nothing from it without capital. Knowing the top is near doesn't help if you must eventually deploy savings.

**Check it yourself:** Search Google Trends for 'this time is different' and compare search volume during November 2021 (Bitcoin peak) versus November 2022 (post-crash). The phrase should peak before crashes, not after.
